{%- endmacro %} {% macro more_info(id) -%} {%- endmacro %} {% macro definitions(id) -%} {# What the numbers mean (Calculations table, D23, D26). #}
Total / Account Value
Cash plus the market value of every stock held (shares × current price).
Day Change
How much the stocks' value moved since the previous trading day's close. The percentage is of the value at that close.
Year to date Change
Value now, minus the value on 1 January, minus money deposited or moved in this year (plus money taken out), so deposits don't count as gains. The percentage is of the start-of-year value plus the money put in.
Cost Basis
What the shares cost: shares × average price paid.
Gain/Loss
Market value minus cost basis. The percentage is of the cost basis.
