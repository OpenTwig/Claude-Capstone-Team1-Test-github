Total / Account Value
Cash plus what every stock is worth (shares × current price).
Day Change
How much the stocks' value moved since yesterday's close.
Year to date Change
The value now, minus the value on 1 January, minus any money put in since then, so deposits don't count as gains.
Cost Basis
What the shares cost: shares × the average price paid.
Gain/Loss
What the shares are worth now, minus what they cost.
